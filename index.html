SELLING ROLES

The two selling roles
Brand Representative Recommended
Selling Role
A first-party seller or authorised partner with full access to all brand marketing tools — including Brand Analytics and Manage Your Experiments.
Reseller
Selling Role
An external third-party distributor with limited brand tool access. Cannot view Brand Analytics or run listing experiments on behalf of the brand.
Note: Selling roles are separate from protection roles (Administrator, Rights Owner, Registered Agent). A single account can hold both a selling role and a protection role simultaneously — for example, Pattern can hold Brand Representative and Registered Agent for the same brand without conflict.
